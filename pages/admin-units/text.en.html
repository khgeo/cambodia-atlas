Cambodia is divided into the capital, Phnom Penh, and 24 provinces. These are divided into 209 districts: 163 srok, 32 krong (municipalities) and the 14 khan of Phnom Penh. Districts are divided into 1,653 communes: 1,387 khum and 266 sangkat. The communes contain 14,578 villages (phum).

Switch levels to see how the units nest, colour the provinces by population, density or area, and search any place by its Khmer or English name. Zoom in past level 9 to see village points.

About the data. Boundaries and village points are the 2024 administrative dataset, shown at full detail as vector tiles. Areas are computed from the boundaries in UTM zone 48N, without the Tonle Sap lake. Population is from the 2024 Inter-censal Population Survey; check it against the NIS report before you cite it.
