Phnom Penh's public bus service began as a trial in September 2014 and launched officially in October 2015. By the 2020 route map it ran 13 lines (17 including branches A, B and C) from 05:30 to 20:30, for an adult fare of 1,500 riel. Monks, students, teachers, garment workers, people over 70 and wheelchair users ride free.
