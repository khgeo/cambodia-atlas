Phnom Penh's public bus service began as a trial in September 2014 and launched officially in October 2015. By the 2020 route map it ran 13 lines (17 including branches A, B and C) from 05:30 to 20:30, for an adult fare of 1,500 riel. Monks, students, teachers, garment workers, people over 70 and wheelchair users ride free.

How the times are estimated. The brochure gives only operating hours and how often buses leave (every 10–15, 15–20 or 20–30 minutes). Arrival times at each stop are worked out from the distance along the route, an average speed and a short stop at each bus stop. Change the speed below to see how traffic would change the timetable. Routes follow the 2014 road network (MPWT) through the roads named on the route map. Line 6 returns along Win Win Blvd, which is newer than the road data, so that part is drawn straight. Stops are in the right order but spaced evenly along each route, so their exact positions are approximate.
